Talas Gendarmerie Academy

The officer school of the Gendarmerie. Officers of the city police and of the district detachments train here too.

CoursesTwo-year and four-year courses in law, forensic investigation, border control and mounted patrol
Who may applyResidents of the Zone aged 18 to 25 who have completed twelfth grade and military service, or are exempt from it
TestsWritten test in Iradi and English, physical test, medical examination and a background check
Applications for 2027Until 1 March 2027, in person at the Academy in Talas
