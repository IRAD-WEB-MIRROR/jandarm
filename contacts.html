Contacts and reception

In an emergency on the highway or at the line, call the duty officer at any hour. Written appeals are answered within 30 days. Anonymous reports of smuggling and fuel theft are accepted; other anonymous appeals are not considered.

District commands and posts

Each district command keeps a duty officer round the clock and receives citizens on weekdays, 9:00–13:00. Chu and Talas districts are served from Nurzhan and from headquarters in Talas.
