Documents and legal acts

The acts and directives the Gendarmerie enforces, and the Commander's orders that bind the public.

Order of the Commander · 20 October 2026 · JG-41

On winter equipment of vehicles on State Route 1, 2026–27

Chains or winter tyres compulsory at the Fergana Gateway post from 1 November 2026 to 31 March 2027; the post may close the road when the avalanche danger is 4 or higher.

Order of the Commander · 28 October 2024 · JG-17

On photography at checkpoints and protected installations

Prohibits photographing or filming checkpoints, convoys and protected installations, and sets out how equipment is held and images deleted.

Executive directive · 17 May 2021 · 2021/09

On the Gendarmerie register of fuel tankers

Every fuel tanker on the roads of the Zone is registered with the Gendarmerie and re-registered every year; unregistered tankers are held at the checkpoints.

Executive directive · 8 April 2014 · 2014/22

On the carriage of dangerous goods on State Route 1

Dangerous goods pass the Otmek tunnel only between 22:00 and 05:00, under Highway Patrol escort.

Executive directive · 11 February 2008 · 2008/03

On drug testing of drivers at the checkpoints

Drivers of goods vehicles and passenger minibuses crossing the line may be tested; a driver who refuses is treated as under the influence.

Act · 14 May 1993 · № 93-12

Republican Gendarmerie Act

Gives the Gendarmerie its statutory basis: the protection of the frontiers and the line, of state installations and senior officials, highway policing, and assistance to the city police and district detachments on request. As amended in 2007 and 2009.
