Republican Gendarmerie

The Republican Gendarmerie guards the Qara-Chik line and the frontier crossings, patrols the highways, protects state installations and senior officials, and keeps order where the city police and the district detachments call for it. It answers to the Ministry of Internal Security.

Photographing checkpoints, convoys and protected installations is prohibited. Instructions given by a gendarme at a checkpoint must be followed at once.

Reporting an offence

Smuggling, fuel theft, unregistered tankers and suspicious loads may be reported to the duty officer at duty@jandarm.iradgov.org or at any checkpoint. Reports may be made without giving a name.
