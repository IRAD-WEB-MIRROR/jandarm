Leadership

The Commander of the Republican Gendarmerie is appointed by the Governor on the proposal of the Minister of Internal Security and answers to the Minister.

Major General Ruslan Toktarov

Major General Ruslan Toktarov

Commander of the Republican Gendarmerie · since 2012

Born in 1978. Graduated from the Omsk Higher Tank Command School in 1999 and served in the Gendarmerie from 2000, commanding the Highway Patrol Corps before his appointment as Commander in March 2012. Under his command the Gendarmerie took over the internal posts of the corridor, opened the register of fuel tankers and moved the Academy to four-year courses.
