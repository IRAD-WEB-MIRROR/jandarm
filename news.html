Press service news

Notices of the Republican Gendarmerie and its press office, newest first.

2026

  • 26 October 2026
    Winter equipment on State Route 1 from 1 November

    From 1 November to 31 March every vehicle passing the Fergana Gateway post must carry chains or have winter tyres. Vehicles without them are turned back. See roads.

  • 19 October 2026
    Register of fuel tankers: annual re-registration

    Owners of fuel tankers must re-register them by 30 November 2026. Unregistered tankers will not be allowed through the checkpoints, and fines are doubled after 30 days.

  • 5 October 2026
    Academy: applications for 2027

    Applications for the courses beginning in September 2027 are accepted at the Academy in Talas until 1 March 2027. See the Academy.

  • 20 August 2026
    Feast of St. Janyl: pilgrims' crossings

    From 22 to 25 August the Qara-Chik and Kara-Bulak checkpoints stay open until midnight for pilgrims travelling to Iyık Janyl, with UNMI liaison officers present. Pilgrims from the tribal regions cross on a clan travel permit…

  • 17 July 2026
    Academy graduation

    184 cadets of the Talas Gendarmerie Academy received the rank of lieutenant on the parade ground today, 41 of them for the city police and the district detachments. The Minister of Internal Security presented the diplomas.

  • 24 June 2026
    Wild hemp: summer clearance on the lower Chu

    The Gendarmerie and the Chu District detachment have begun the annual destruction of wild hemp on the lower Chu. Residents are reminded that gathering or processing wild hemp is a criminal offence.

  • 11 March 2026
    Irad Day: road closures in Talas

    On 11 March Central Square and the approaches to Government House are closed to traffic from 6:00 to 14:00. Bags may be searched at the entrances to the square.

2025

  • 27 October 2025
    Winter equipment on State Route 1, 2025–26

    The winter equipment rule applies from 1 November 2025 to 31 March 2026. Last winter the Highway Patrol turned back 2,316 vehicles at the Fergana Gateway post.

  • 18 July 2025
    Academy graduation

    176 lieutenants graduated from the Academy, the first class to complete the four-year course in law and forensic investigation.

2024

  • 4 November 2024
    Photography at checkpoints

    Photographing or filming checkpoints, convoys and protected installations is prohibited. Cameras and telephones used to do so are held and the images deleted in the presence of the owner.

2022

  • 19 January 2022
    State Route 1 reopened after avalanches

    The highway between the Otmek post and the tunnel's north portal, closed since 16 January after three avalanches, is open again to cars and light vans. Lorries are let through in convoys under escort at 10:00 and 15:00.

2019

  • 4 July 2019
    Drug checks at the line: the 2019 figures

    In the first half of 2019 the checkpoints of the Border Demarcation Brigade tested 3,912 drivers and found 214 under the influence of drugs. Drug tests have been carried out at the checkpoints since 2008.
