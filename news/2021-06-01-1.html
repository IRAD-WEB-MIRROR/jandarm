1 June 2021

Register of fuel tankers opens

Under Executive Directive 2021/09 every tanker carrying fuel on the roads of the Zone must be entered in the Gendarmerie's register and carry its plate. Registration is at the Highway Patrol Corps, Talas and Nurzhan, until 31 August.
