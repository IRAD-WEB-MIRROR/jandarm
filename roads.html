Roads

The Highway Patrol Corps enforces the traffic and customs rules on the Fergana Mountain Transit Highway (State Route 1) and on the roads to the northern crossings.

Otmek tunnel: dangerous goods

Fuel, explosives, gas and chemicals may pass through the tunnel only between 22:00 and 05:00. Outside those hours tankers wait at the Otmek valley post or take the old pass road in summer.

Rules on State Route 1

Otmek tunnel, dangerous goodsOnly between 22:00 and 05:00 (executive directive, 2014).
Fuel tankersMust be entered in the Gendarmerie's register of tankers (since 2021). Unregistered tankers are detained with their load.
ConvoysFuel and antimony convoys travel under escort. Do not overtake an escorted convoy.
Road-use chargeHeavy vehicles pay the road-use charge at the Otmek valley post.
BusesPassengers' identity documents are checked at the Ala-Bel summit post.
Toktogul damStopping on the dam road is prohibited.

Winter

From 1 November to 31 March vehicles crossing the Fergana Gateway must carry chains or winter tyres. The pass may be closed without notice in snow; follow the forecasts of Irad Gidromet. The Fergana Gateway is closed by snow on about 20 days in an average winter.

Passes today

Worked out from the observations of Irad Gidromet at Talas. A closure is announced only by the Highway Patrol Corps.

Fines

Traffic fines may be paid at Talasbank or through e-Irad. A fine not paid within 30 days is doubled.
