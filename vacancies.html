Vacancies

The Gendarmerie recruits all year. Contract service is open to residents of the Zone aged 19 to 30 who have completed military service. Apply in person at the personnel office or at any district detachment, with your passport and military record.

Open competitions

RG-26/34

Highway Patrol inspectors

Place of work
Otmek post and Fergana Gateway
Applications until
1 December 2026

Driving licence of categories B and C, three years' driving; rotation of fifteen days on, fifteen off at altitude. Mountain allowance 30%.

RG-26/36

English language instructor (civilian)

Place of work
Talas Gendarmerie Academy
Applications until
20 December 2026

Degree in English; teaching experience. From the spring term, 18 hours a week. Salary 3,300 som a month.

RG-26/31

Gendarmes, contract service

Place of work
Border Demarcation Brigade, the line and the internal posts
Applications until
31 March 2027

Men and women aged 19 to 30 after military service, physically fit, with no criminal record. Three-year contract; pay 2,800 som a month plus the line allowance, uniform, meals on duty and a place in the hostel.
